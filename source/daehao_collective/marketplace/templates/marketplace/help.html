How can we help?

A little guidance for shopping with independent labels.

How does shopping across labels work? +

Browse clothing from multiple independent labels, select a size and colour, and add each piece to your bag. Your bag groups pieces by label and combines them into one checkout. Each label then fulfils its portion separately.

How do retailer deliveries work? +

This demonstration supports Singapore delivery addresses with six-digit postal codes. Delivery is included in displayed prices. Each retailer portion has its own Processing, Shipped or Delivered status. We do not simulate courier tracking events.

What does a recommendation mean? +

If you choose to complete your profile, a trained model ranks products using height, weight, preferred styles and product attributes. The model was trained on synthetic data. A recommendation helps discovery; it does not guarantee fit, a correct size or a purchase preference. Shared-style labels identify matching tags.

If your profile is incomplete, the model is unavailable, an input is unsupported, or nothing is in stock, we explain the situation and provide a labelled curated selection where possible.

Your optional saved gender sets the starting clothing collection: menswear plus unisex for Man, womenswear plus unisex for Woman, and all collections for Non-binary or Prefer not to say. The style model ranks pieces within that collection. Use Clothing selection on For You to explore another collection without changing your profile.

How do I choose a size? +

Save your height and weight in your profile to see an estimated size on For You and product pages. Style preferences are optional for this estimate. Size estimates use synthetic example data; confirm the retailer’s measurements because fit is not guaranteed.

Read the size guidance supplied on each product page. Size labels and available size-colour combinations come from that label's inventory. Product-specific garment measurements are shown only when supplied. Check your exact combination before adding it to your bag.

If your estimated size is sold out or not offered, we show that exact result. We do not suggest a different size just because it is in stock.

How do demonstration payments work? +

There are no real card fields and no payment provider. “Place demo order” simulates an approved payment and records an order. “Simulate declined payment” creates no order, changes no stock and keeps your bag intact. No money is transferred.

When can I cancel an order? +

You can cancel the entire order while every retailer portion remains Processing and the demo payment is Paid. Confirm cancellation on your order detail page. The application restores purchased stock once and labels the simulated payment Refunded. Cancellation is unavailable after any retailer portion ships.

Does adding to my bag reserve stock? +

No. Your bag uses current prices and availability. Checkout checks stock, product activity and retailer status again. If anything changes after review, you must review the updated information before placing an order.

How do I clear my personalisation? +

Open My Profile and choose Clear Personalisation. After confirmation, this removes saved gender, measurements and style preferences. Your account, addresses, bag and previous orders remain available.

Can my clothing label join {{ site_name }}? +

Submit a seller application with your store information. A marketplace administrator reviews applications before sellers can list products. Listings are free; the agreed commission reduces the seller's estimated revenue and is not an extra customer fee.
